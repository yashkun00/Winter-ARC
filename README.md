# ❄️ ARC — Winter ARC

> **A 90-day discipline system for people who want to improve themselves, build consistency, and stay accountable.**

ARC is a personal productivity and self-improvement application built around the **Winter ARC** concept.

Instead of trying to change everything at once, ARC focuses on a small set of daily rules that users follow consistently throughout the Winter ARC.

The goal is simple:

**Show up every day. Build discipline. Track your progress. Become better.**

---

## ✨ What is Winter ARC?

Winter ARC is a 3-month period focused on personal development, discipline, and consistency.

ARC turns that idea into an interactive application where users can:

- Track their daily habits
- Follow the 6 core Winter ARC rules
- Monitor their consistency
- View their progress across 3 months
- Build discipline through daily repetition

The application is designed for people who genuinely want to improve and are willing to stay consistent.

---

## 🎯 The 6 Rules

ARC is built around six daily rules.

| # | Rule | Daily Goal |
|---|------|------------|
| 01 | 😴 Sleep | **7+ hours** |
| 02 | 🏋️ Exercise | **30 minutes** |
| 03 | 📚 Study | **2 hours** |
| 04 | 🚫 No Porn | Maintain discipline |
| 05 | 📖 Reading | **10 pages** |
| 06 | ☀️ Morning Sunlight | **30 minutes** |

The purpose isn't perfection.

The purpose is **consistency**.

## 🚀 Core Features

### ✅ Daily Habit Tracking

Record whether you completed each of the six daily rules.

### 📊 Progress Visualization

See your consistency instead of relying only on motivation.

### 🔥 Discipline-Based System

ARC is designed around repeated daily actions rather than short-term motivation.

### 🗓️ Calendar View

Review previous days and identify your consistency throughout the Winter ARC.

### 🎯 Simple Goals

Six clearly defined rules make the system easy to understand and follow.

---

## 🧠 The Philosophy Behind ARC

Most productivity systems focus heavily on:

> "What do you want to achieve?"

ARC focuses on another question:

> **"What are you willing to do every day?"**

The application is based on the idea that meaningful improvement comes from **small actions repeated consistently**.

You don't need to completely transform your life in one day.

You need to:

**Start → Repeat → Track → Improve.**
